# 0270 — The maintainer's DualShock 4 session on the exp 0267 build: every rumble came from the vibration-port fallback, the replug kept the rumble, and the pad-speaker port held the audio thread 0 ms instead of 1.1 s

**Question.** Exp 0267 proposed a diff (the rumble write on its own thread with a raw-joystick fallback and a one-shot failure log, the pad-speaker port on the default sink made non-blocking, `KYTY_BC5_PAD_SPEAKER=off`) and left three things to a session with the DualShock 4: which path drives the pad's rumble (the F214 vibration-port fallback or classic `scePadSetVibration`), whether the rumble survives an unplug and replug, and whether the pad-speaker port still holds the title's audio thread (exp 0268: 493 + 635 ms at t 149–150 s of the 16:05 session, F326). Does the build with the diff answer them, and does the maintainer feel a difference?

**Setup.**
- Build (`build0267.sh`, `build0267.py`, exp 0252's pattern): the shared normal build's two units `audio.cpp` and `controller.cpp` copied from `~/src/KytyPlus` (md5 `7c4210a5`, `7551b768`; tree HEAD f266548 with patch 0001 applied, `bc5LleAgc.cpp` `daca24cc`, `libSaveData.cpp` `cafb1a54`), the exp 0267 diff applied to the copies (`03b3af4e`, `4d2d4318`; `controller.cpp` is CRLF on the box, so the diff went on with `--ignore-whitespace` and the copy kept CRLF), compiled with the shared build's exact flags into `~/bc5-work/exp0267/obj` and relinked against the shared build's other objects into `~/bc5-work/exp0267/kyty_emulator`, md5 `687eb84c` (16:54, 18 s). The shared normal binary stayed `8c63d974` before and after; nothing under the shared build or tree was written.
- Session (`play-ds4.sh`): the second title through `play-pkg.sh` with `BC5_EMU` pointing at the private binary and `KYTY_BC5_INPUT_TRACE=1 KYTY_BC5_AUDIO_TRACE=1 KYTY_BC5_MEASURE=1`, started by the top session over ssh as a user unit at 16:55:06 for the maintainer, with the DualShock 4 (no DualSense); the maintainer unplugged and replugged the pad during play; exit 0 at 17:00:40 (335 s). The live saves were copied first by the top session (`~/bc5-work/exp0267/saves-before-ds4-1655`). Log `~/bc5-work/exp0267/play-ds4-1655.log` (3.9 MB, 35,499 lines), copied once to the laptop and read there; `raw/play-ds4-1655-extract.txt` holds the extracted lines. - The maintainer's words afterwards (via the top session): "Działa poprawnie. Odłączenie i podłączenie ok. Nie było spowolnień" (works correctly, unplug and replug OK, no slowdowns).

**Result.**
1. **Every rumble write came from the F214 vibration-port fallback.** 4,516 `input trace: rumble L/S for D ms via PATH -> rc` lines, all with `D = 100 ms` (the fallback's duration), none with `5,000 ms` (a classic `scePadSetVibration` call). So with a DualShock 4 the second title streams its haptics to a vibration port exactly as with a DualSense, the port has no SDL device, and `PadRumbleFromVibrationFrames` → `ControllerAudioRumble` → (now) the worker thread → `SDL_GameControllerRumble` is the only thing that moves the pad's motors. 4,304 writes carried a non-zero value, 212 a zero (the 80 ms release); the first at t = 41.6 s (the game, after the title screen and a 10.4 s load with no audio at 32.9–43.2 s), then 15 writes/s on average through to t = 328.6 s, with the gaps of a pause or a quiet scene (12.9 s at 53–66 s, 11.2 s at 70–81 s, 9.2 s at 102–111 s; `raw/`, "gaps"). This settles exp 0267's result 1: `KYTY_BC5_PAD_RUMBLE_FALLBACK=0` would switch this title's DualShock 4 rumble off entirely.
2. **The replug kept the rumble, through the game-controller path.** All 4,516 writes went `via game controller` with `rc 0`; no `rumble: pad instance …` failure line, so the diff's raw-joystick fallback and slot diagnostics were never needed in this session. The replug is in the log as the title's re-initialisation of the pad: port 1, a second 8-channel main port until t = 32.9 s, reopens as the pad-speaker port (type 4, 1 ch) at t = 155.5 s, exactly as it did at t = 82.9 s of the 16:05 session after that replug; the fps counter has its dip right before (t 144–147 s: 32, 11, 19, 32 flips/s, gaps to 296 ms, the hotplug and re-initialisation) and the rumble stream runs on through it (gap 145.4 → 149.1 s, then 175–240 writes per 10 s for the rest of the session). The lost rumble of 2026-10-24 16:05 did not reproduce, so which of exp 0267's candidates caused it (a raw-joystick reopen, a slot-1 bind) stays undecided; on this build either would have logged a `rumble:` line.
3. **The pad-speaker port held the audio thread 0.0 ms.** Port 1 as type 4 has 178 one-second trace lines from t = 155.5 to 333.0 s, every one with `held 0.0 ms`, `0 clears`, rate 1.000 after the first partial second, `sdl queued 0-0 bytes`, "device playing": the non-blocking default-sink port (`PortOut::pad_nb`) queues and never waits, and the queue never grew to the clear threshold. Against F326's 493 and 635 ms in two consecutive seconds on the previous build, the hold that cost the main mix 0.36 s is gone. The main port shows one dip after the replug, t = 161.5 s (rate 0.777, held 649 ms, queue drained to 0, a 542 ms frame gap at 160.9 s): about 0.22 s of main mix missing in one second, with the pad-speaker port at 0 ms hold in that same second, so it is not the port's wait; two more mild seconds at 234.7 s (rate 0.947) and 292.9 s (rate 1.000, held 863 ms) and nothing else below 0.95 in 290 seconds of main-port trace.
4. **Frames.** 14–15 flips/s during the 35–44 s load and the 58–61 s window, dips to 11 flips/s at 105.6 s (gap 380 ms), 14 at 113.8 s (237 ms) and 11 at 145.4 s (296 ms, the replug), 27 at 160.9 s (542 ms); after t = 170 s the median is 40 flips/s with a minimum of 14 (161 seconds). The 16:05 session had the same shape (14–15 flips/s during its unplug and replug windows). The maintainer reports no slowdowns on this build; the fps counter cannot separate the pad events from the scenes, and nothing here is attributed to the rumble write, which no longer runs on a title thread.

**Verdict.** Passed for what a session can show: the rumble of the second title with a DualShock 4 is entirely the F214 fallback (4,516 writes, all 100 ms, none classic), the rumble survived the unplug and replug with every write accepted through the game-controller path, and the pad-speaker port's hold on the audio thread went from 1.1 s in two seconds (F326) to 0 ms in all 178 seconds it was open. The maintainer felt no slowdown. Not shown: the earlier loss of rumble after a replug did not reproduce, so its cause is still open (the diff would log it next time); the one 0.22 s main-mix dip at t = 161.5 s is unexplained and not the port's wait. Recommendation: integrate the exp 0267 diff into patch 0001 (the worker thread, the non-blocking pad-speaker port by default, `KYTY_BC5_PAD_SPEAKER=main-blocking`/`off`), with the `rumble:` and `input trace: rumble` lines kept; F214 is verified on the box for the second title with a DualShock 4 in play, through the fallback.
