# 0405 — Port research for any title (exp 0402 redone without the title gate)

**Question.** Which fixes and features of the related open-source projects should our host take, if the host must be
complete and correct for any title and not only for our two? Each candidate gets its effort, its risk, its licence and
its source (path and commit). It is marked **e4-now** (fits the current private test build: small, sourced,
CPU-testable, no GPU submission by default) or **later**.

The rule is the maintainer's, of 2026-11-03 23:0x: "nie patrz poprawek/portów z innych projektów tylko pod kątem naszych
dwóch gier, bo będziesz wszystko odrzucać". It came with "sesja wody ma powtórzyć research skoro robiła wcześniej takie
ograniczenie": exp 0402 had judged ports by whether our titles show a symptom, and this experiment redoes it.

Under the rule there are only two valid skips:

- "we already match", with our file:line;
- "the licence or provenance is not acceptable".

**Setup.** Read-only research on the laptop, 2026-11-03 23:2x to 2026-11-04 01:16, by three agents of the water side
session (exp 0401). Nothing was built, run, ported or committed during the research. There was no box access and no
Sony material. Ours is the e4 tree: KytyPlus `f266548` with patch 0001, the same tree exp 0401's series e4 builds on.

*Decisions for the maintainer* (nothing in them was ported):

1. **Firmware modules run natively or under an emulator** (AnyPS5's BunnyFl runs, prosper's readings of 13.42 modules)
   are not console measurements. Accept them as sources, or exclude them like disassembly? R1 kept four items out of
   e4-now for this: Rtc year handling, the Rudp event handler, the Net resolver timeout and the Ngs2 PCM loops.
2. **Three values read from titles' own `libc.prx`** were classed as excluded (R1). Confirm.
3. **AnyPS5's JIT shared-memory signatures** come from an IDA type database (R3 M31). Borderline; not ported.
4. **The 32 unloaded tables** (R1 #5): load them as they are, port first, or leave them dead, per table.
5. **`SET_PREDICATION`** (R3 G7) changes GPU submission when its switch is on. CLAUDE.md asks first.
6. **Our tree:** upstream KytyPlus `f266548`'s `libs/ngs2_pcm.h:7` says its values come from "psOff / SDK dumps"
   (R2 side finding). It is included nowhere, like `ngs2_vag_decoder.h` and `audio3d_impl.inc`. Keep these files out of
   every build. Do not reuse their values in an NGS2 port.

  use-after-frees, hangs and silent corruption that any title can hit.

and reviewed, and told the top session so. What comes next is the top session's call:
