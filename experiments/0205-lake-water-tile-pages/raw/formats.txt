Reading:
- The pixel stage samples two streamed single-channel maps (BC4 and half float). The exp 0201/0205 samplers used an
  8 MiB / 2 MiB window, wider than the textures (2.7 MiB / 0.7 MiB with their chains).
- The vertex stage samples a 2048x2048 half-float map and a 512x512 RGBA half-float map whose sizes are exactly
  their automatic maps (GPU-only, prot 0x30, mapped at the title start). In A1 both were all zero from the title to
  the end, on the pages the GPU itself uses (AMM views are imported eagerly, ADR 0006).
- A lead, TODO(verify): a missing writer for those two maps. A water or ripple simulation at rest would still write
  its rest state, and for an RGBA16F map of normals or flow that is typically non-zero, so all zero from the title
  to the end leans toward a GPU pass whose output never lands there rather than calm water. Not established: the
  maps' meaning is not known, and no writer has been looked for in a journal of descriptor tables.
