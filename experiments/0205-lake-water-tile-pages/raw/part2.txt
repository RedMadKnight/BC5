Verdict of the A/B: no effect of KYTY_BC5_APR_READAHEAD on the patchwork. Both arms have wholly clean runs and runs with
patches; the spread between runs is far larger than any difference between the arms. The read-ahead is not a water fix.
(Patches that come and go within seconds in the frozen view were seen in B1b and B2 only; with 2-3 runs per arm that is
not a difference.)
The 8 MiB range at X2's address of the BC4 texture (0x8492504000) per run, res0205.py on the last tile map before the
emulator's exit (raw/part2-ana.txt and the pages files):
  A2, A3 (clean): the 2048x2048 BC4 texture's whole chain resident: mip 0 500 / 512 of 512 blocks, mips 1-4 all blocks.
  X5 (patchy 18-23 %): mips 4 and 3 resident, mip 2 half, mips 1 and 0 nothing.
  A1 (patchy), B1b, B2, B3: a few scattered blocks only (8, 16, 63, 4 blocks): the texture was probably elsewhere.
So in the two clean runs whose texture sat at X2's address it was fully resident, and in the patchy one at that address
its two finest mips were missing. A lead for "flat cell = a page of this texture that the title did not stream", not
established: the address was not re-read in these runs (no journal), and the runs where it moved say nothing.
The AMM pool the title gives (AmmGiveDirectMemory, the AMM trace's "give direct memory") is the same in every logged run:
0xbda00000 bytes at 0x99200000 in tcpW1, X3 and S2. The pool size is not what varies between clean and patchy runs.
